Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the local Threads community-management integration operated by the account owner of github.com/buynyakov handles information accessed through Meta’s official Threads API.

Information accessed

The integration may access the connected Threads account’s profile identifier, username, public profile information, posts, replies, mentions, public reply metadata, and account or post insights. It may also receive webhook notifications about replies on the connected account’s Threads posts.

How information is used

Information is used only to manage the connected Threads presence: monitor replies and mentions, classify routine interactions, respond to eligible comments, moderate obvious spam or severe abuse, and measure post performance. It is not used for advertising, credit decisions, surveillance, or selling data.

Storage and sharing

Operational state and analytics are stored locally on the account owner’s computer. Access tokens and configuration secrets are stored in access-restricted local files. Threads data is not sold or shared with data brokers. The integration uses Meta’s official Threads API and does not use password-based automation, browser scraping, or private endpoints.

Retention

Interaction state and aggregate analytics are retained only as needed to prevent duplicate actions and analyze account performance. The account owner may delete the local state and analytics at any time. Access can be revoked through Threads account settings or Meta’s connected-app controls; local credentials and stored data are then removed during cleanup.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of data associated with this integration, contact the operator through the public profile at github.com/buynyakov and identify the request as a Threads data-deletion request. Do not post access tokens, passwords, or other sensitive information publicly. The operator will delete the relevant local state and stored data and confirm completion through a private contact channel available through that profile.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the integration’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a material change is made.
